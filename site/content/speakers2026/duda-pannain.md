Duda Pannain is a Brazilian Actress, Producer and Creator based in Rio de Janeiro, with experience in both theater and audiovisual productions. Throughout her career, she has performed in 9 theater plays, 6 short films, 1 medium-length film, 2 feature films and 6 web series. She is also the Creator, Producer and Lead Actress of the comedy web series “The Sun is for Everyone” (O Sol é Para Todes), an award-winning project recognized at international festivals. Her creative background also includes experience in screenwriting, assistant directing, costume design and art direction. Duda holds a degree in International Relations, which contributes to her artistic perspective and interest in collaborative and cross-cultural storytelling.
