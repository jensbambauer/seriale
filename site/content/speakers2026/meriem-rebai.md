Meriem Rebai is a Film Director and Founder of reelistiq, Germany’s first agency for Vertical Drama. With over twelve years in the film industry – including roles at Filmakademie Baden-Württemberg and kurhaus production, where she worked on cinema and TV productions – she brings a deep narrative background to the world of branded content. At reelistiq, she develops fictional series formats for premium brands, exploring how brands can build long-term cultural relevance through storytelling.
