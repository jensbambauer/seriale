A Chilean Actress and Writer, Amarú has built the majority of her filmmaking career in Spain, where she migrated in 2021. Since 2024, she has lived in Germany, where she has worked as an Actress, Producer, and Host of die Seriale. She is currently in the writing phase of her first feature film.
