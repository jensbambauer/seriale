Ash Finn is a Writer, Production Designer, and Art Director for “Ren: The Girl with the Mark” Season 3. Previous writing credits include “Ren: The Girl with the Mark” Season 2, for which Ash was also a Prop Maker and Standby Props. Other art department work includes short film “Personal Bests” (2026), and Season 1 of “Ren: The Girl with the Mark” (2015). They were an Associate Producer for “The Second Shepherd’s Play” (2016).
