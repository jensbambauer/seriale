Lada Nevmyatullina, a pianist, organist and conductor who has performed throughout Germany, began her musical education at a municipal music school in the heart of Russia. She completed her master's degree at the “Hochschule für Musik und Darstellende Kunst” in Frankfurt am Main. The scholarship holder of the “Akademie der Wissenschaften und der Literatur” in Mainz gave numerous concerts with “Philharmonie Südwestfalen”, “Göttinger Symphonie Orchester” and “Neubrandenburger Philharmonie”, among others, and was also a participant of the “Lille Piano(s) Festival” in France.
