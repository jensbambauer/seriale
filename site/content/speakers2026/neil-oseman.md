Neil has been a Cinematographer for over 25 years and his feature credits as such include the 2024 Hamlet adaptation starring Sir Ian McKellen. He joined the crew of Kate Madison and Christopher Dane’s fantasy web series “Ren: The Girl with the Mark” as Director of Photography and subsequently took over as Showrunner for seasons two and three. Once described by The Guardian as “The Spielberg of Hereford,” Neil’s previous forays into producing indie film include his fantasy-action feature “Soul Searcher” and a proof-of-concept short starring Benedict Cumberbatch.
