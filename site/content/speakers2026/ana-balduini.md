Ana is an Actress graduated from the National University of the Arts (UNA) in Buenos Aires. She complemented with artists and teachers such as Maricel Álvarez, Paula Grinzpan, and Teatro Bravard. She has developed a career within Buenos Aires’ independent and official theatre scene, with notable works including “Clase Póstuma,” directed by Alejandro Robino at Teatro San Martín (2024), nominated for the ACE Awards; “El juego de la silla” by Ana Katz; “Potranca, el galope de la historia” by Asensio; and “Posiblemente un fracaso” by Morgado. She is currently part of Guardianes de los dioses, a co-production between Teatro San Martín and UngKlara (Sweden), which premiered in Buenos Aires after a rehearsal process in Turkey. In film and television, she has worked on “Venus en juego,” “Leni,” and “Real.” In 2026, she will premiere “Franquicieros” on Prime Video. For more than ten years she has worked in cultural management, executive production, and casting production for theatre and audiovisual projects.
