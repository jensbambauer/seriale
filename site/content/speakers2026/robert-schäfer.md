Born and raised in Heidelberg, he studied Film Studies and Philosophy in Mainz before completing a second Bachelor’s degree in Motion Pictures in Darmstadt. After gaining experience across various areas of the media industry, he worked as a Marketing Manager for a personnel consultancy in Frankfurt am Main. Alongside his professional career, he has continuously worked as a freelance Moderator and Host. Since April 2026, he has been part of the news editorial team at Antenne Mainz, where he works as a News Presenter.
