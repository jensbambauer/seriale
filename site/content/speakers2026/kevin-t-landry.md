Kevin T. Landry is a Montreal-based Director, Screenwriter & Editor interested in the imaginary, the absurd, and the fine line that separates the worlds of reality and fantasy. He now has over 15 short films and two full-length features to his name, films that have travelled all over the word and won numerous prizes.
