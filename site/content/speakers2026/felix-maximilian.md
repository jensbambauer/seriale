Felix Maximilian is a German Actor based in Berlin and Gran Canaria. He works across film, television, and theater in German, English, Spanish, and French. He appeared in the hit Spanish Netflix series “Machos Alfa” as well as the feature film “Sicilian Holiday” (Amazon Prime). Additionally, he has starred in numerous well-known German TV productions in both leading and supporting roles, including “Die Notärztin” (ARD), “Herz über” Kopf (RTL), and “Lindenstraße” (ARD). Known for a charismatic presence that he skillfully knows how to undercut with subtlety, Felix Maximilian works internationally in film and series productions. He was nominated for “Best Actor” at the Pinerolo Film Festival for the French short film “Hirsingen.” In addition to his acting career, he has co-produced both a documentary and a feature film.
