Lucía Zin Ungaro (1996) is an Argentine Director and Screenwriter. Her short film “Álvaro Álvarez” was premiered at Festival de Cine Inusual (2024). She wrote and directed the documentary series “Masters of Medicine”, which premiered on TV Pública (2024/2025). She works across national audiovisual productions as part of the direction team, with credits including “Argentina, 1985” (Amazon), “The Substitute” (Netflix), “Puan” (Amazon), and “The Man Who Loved Flying Saucers” (Netflix). She is currently presenting the series “I Have Something to Share with You” at die Seriale.
