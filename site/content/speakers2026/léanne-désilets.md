Léanne Désilets is a young Quebecer who began her career as an Actress at the age of 15. Since then, she has taken on a series of notable projects, including the film “Jeune Juliette” by Anne Émond in 2018, the series “Mukbang” in 2025, and several other leading roles in television series. Winner of the Gémeaux Award in 2022 for her supporting role in “La vie compliquée de Léa Olivier,” she also received an Idéa performance award in 2023 for a campaign for the Montreal Heart Institute Foundation. In 2025, she was nominated for a Gémeaux Award for her leading role in the series “Denis Danger.” Alongside her acting career, Léanne is developing a practice in directing and artistic direction. A graduate of Collège Ahuntsic, she is currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in film production at University of Montreal. Her dual artistic and technical approach makes her a versatile emerging creator, actively engaged in the audiovisual industry.
