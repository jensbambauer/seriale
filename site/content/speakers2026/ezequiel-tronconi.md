He directed the films: “The Charm,” “March,” “Outsiders and Elephant,” “What Would You Rather?” (filmed in Spain), “Going” (filmed in Spain, Ireland, Mexico, and Buenos Aires); and the series: “An Ultra-Deformed Walk” (nominated for the 2024 Cóndor de Plata Awards, Best Short Series, Official Competition at the die Seriale and Seriesland) and “DT, the Mission,” winner of the 2024 Martín Fierro Award. He was nominated in the categories of Best Director, Best Actor, and Best Original Screenplay at the Sur Awards of the Argentine Film Academy for the film “The Charm.” He has acted in more than 20 films, 10 of them as the lead. In Spain and Uruguay, he filmed “The Year of Fury” (HBO). and in Portugal, the series “Cuba Libre” (winner of the 2023 Golden Globe Award for Best Series). He starred in the series “The Liars”, (Official Competition at the Canneseries Festival) and won the 2023 Martín Fierro Digital Award.
