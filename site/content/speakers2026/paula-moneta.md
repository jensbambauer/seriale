Paula Moneta studied filmmaking at UNLP and FUC in Buenos Aires, and has further training in AI, photography, and fashion production. She studied screenwriting at Showrunners Barcelona and participated in script labs in Argentina. She directed “Gypsy,” winner of the Emerging Talent Award at the 2018 L.A. Fashion Film Festival, and directed “Memorias,” “Jacket Dealer,” and “The Hate,” screened at major international fashion film festivals like La Jolla, Barcelona, Buenos Aires, Porto, Istanbul, South Africa, London Fashion Film Festivals. Her work includes commercials and digital content for brands, as well as AI-driven projects integrating visual effects. She has directed music videos for Sony Music and independent artists. She created, wrote, directed, and co-produced the mini-series “Venus en Juego,” premiering on Flow in co-production with Flixxo. At the time she is currently in post-production on “Bendiciones” (short horror film) and developing her first feature.
