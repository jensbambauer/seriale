Pilar Germano studied at the National University of Arts (UNA). Professionally, she works as an actress in theater and film. She currently has a play running in Buenos Aires, “Paravincular.” She starred alongside Ezequiel Tronconi in the web series “Un día antes de viajar” (The Day Before Leaving), which premieres on die Seriale and will soon air on the UN3 channel. She also appeared in the films “Yendo” (Going) and “Estación Almagro” (Almagro Station), both directed by Ezequiel Tronconi.
