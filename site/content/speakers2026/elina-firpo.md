Elina Firpo (1996) is an Argentine Director and Screenwriter. Her documentary “En el campo los días son más largos” premiered at BAFICI 2026, and her short film “Ni sí ni no” at BAFICI 2021. She works across Argentine and Spanish audiovisual productions as part of the direction team, with credits including Nada, El Encargado 2, Bellas Artes (Disney+), and Oasis (Netflix). She is currently presenting the series “I have something to share with you” at die Seriale.
