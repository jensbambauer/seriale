Prague in the last months of German occupation: Inspector Beran investigates the murder of a German general’s widow, aided by Buback, whose loyalties remain unclear. A serial killer is targeting Germans and Czechs alike. With the 1945 uprising, the case turns into a reckoning with guilt, revenge, and justice.
