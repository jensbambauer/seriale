For the past three years, Irene has built a distinguished career at the production company A Film To Kill For, developing a versatile profile that bridges the gap between logistical precision and creative vision. As an Assistant Director and Production Manager, she has spearheaded a wide array of projects, ranging from the interactive narrative experience Black Harbour to multiple series including Power Up! (3 seasons), Fetitxe, Mari: Sorgin Ehiztaria, and Papir-oh!. Her expertise extends far beyond the set; Irene is a key contributor to the screenwriting process, specializing in scriptwriting and Basque language dialogues.In the field of cultural management and communication, she has taken on leadership roles in major events, serving as the Organizer for the last two editions of Seriesland, consolidating it as a premier festival for digital series. Additionally, at the Bilbao Surf Film Festival, she served as Head of 360° communication, managing PR, social media, and advertising.
