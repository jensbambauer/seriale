Dipu is the Co-Creator of the internationally award-winning digital series “The Pantsless Detective” and co-manages the Web Series World Cup in the Digital Media World Cups. He has won awards for writing, editing, and directing the series, plus special awards for his contributions to the digital series community from Melbourne Web Fest, Marseille Web Fest, and Minnesota WebFest. The Texas Filmmaker has moderated panels at die Seriale, Bilbao Seriesland (Spain), and Minnesota WebFest (USA), where he was also the 2024 Keynote Speaker. In addition, he has been a Guest Speaker at Cinema Jove (Spain) and a Jury Member for festivals in Australia, Brazil, Germany, Peru, Spain, and the United States (and soon, Indonesia). He has also been a Guest Host on the online pop culture show “Super Geeked Up” and has worked on a variety of independent film projects, including sports and music documentaries.
