Anne Failing studied Media Studies in Marburg, where her passion for storytelling and digital formats began to take shape. She gained hands-on industry experience through her work with Dobago Film, contributing to various web series productions, and through her long-standing involvement with die Seriale. She gained further experience in the television industry in Cologne, deepening her skills in media production and communication. Today, Anne works in communications and public relations. Alongside her full-time role, she follows her passion in storytelling as a freelance wedding speaker.
