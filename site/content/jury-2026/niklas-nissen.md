Niklas Nissen has been a Funding Consultant at Hessen Film & Medien for four years. In this role, he oversees script and treatment development funding and is also responsible for short films within the Talent Film department. Prior to joining Hessen Film & Medien, he spent several years working on various film sets for both cinema and television projects.
