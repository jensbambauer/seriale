French, writes scripts for TV since 1989, lives in Berlin and is a bigtime shortform aficionado. Founded in 2001 the very first (and shortlived) European portal for Flash animated indie cartoons. In 2014, he founded the blog “Web Series Mag” for the online version of French newspaper Liberation. The following year, he founded the Web Series World Cup. Author of “Le guide des Webséries – la nouvelle vague” and Co-author with Meredith Burkholder of “Short Narrative & Serialized, a complete guide of the web series phenomenon.” He is currently Script Doctor, Freelance Consultant in shortform production and distribution strategies and Partner Researcher at University of Wollongong (“Valuing web series”).
