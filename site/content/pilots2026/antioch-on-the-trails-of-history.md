“ANTIOCH – On the Trails of History” is a 30-minute documentary in which Lucas Yalaza travels from Wiesbaden to explore his family’s roots and rediscover the heritage of ancient Antioch. Between ruins and stories, the pilot reflects on identity and the importance of remembering history.
